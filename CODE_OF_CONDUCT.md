# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Standards

Examples of encouraged behavior:

- Respectful technical disagreement grounded in evidence
- Clear reproduction steps and logs when reporting issues
- Consideration of safety implications for biometric, financial, and data-handling features

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Promoting illegal uses of cryptography, surveillance, or biometric tooling

## Reporting

Send reports to [input@arpacorp.net](mailto:input@arpacorp.net), [security@arpacorp.net](mailto:security@arpacorp.net), or [research@arpacorp.net](mailto:research@arpacorp.net), choosing the inbox that best matches your concern. Include timestamps, transcripts (redacted), and platform details where applicable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, lock threads, or ban accounts for violations. Severe cases may be referred to platform operators or legal counsel.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related communications.
